Who Is Oprah Winfrey?

Oprah Winfrey is a talk show host, media executive, actress and billionaire philanthropist. She's best known for being the host of her own, wildly popular program, The Oprah Winfrey Show, which aired for 25 seasons, from 1986 to 2011. In 2011, Winfrey launched her own TV network, the Oprah Winfrey Network (OWN).

Born in the rural town of Kosciusko, Mississippi, Winfrey moved to Baltimore in 1976, where she hosted People Are Talking. Afterward, she was recruited by a Chicago TV station to host her own morning show.

Who Is Naomi Osaka?

Naomi Osaka is a Japanese tennis player. She is a four-time champion of the Grand Slam, the biggest tournaments in tennis. In 2019, she became the first Asian player to be number one in the world. She is also known for talking about mental health and fairness for all people.

Born in the city of Osaka, Japan, she moved to the United States with her family when she was three years old. Her father trained her and her sister Mari to play tennis. In 2018, Osaka won the U.S. Open and became the first Japanese player to win a Grand Slam title. After that, she took a break from tennis to take care of her health. In 2023, she had a daughter named Shai, and in 2024 she came back to play.

Who Was Maya Angelou?

Maya Angelou was an American poet, writer, actress and activist. She is best known for her memoir I Know Why the Caged Bird Sings (1969). It was a bestseller, and it was the first nonfiction bestseller by a Black woman. Her poems, like Still I Rise, are famous around the world.

Born Marguerite Ann Johnson in St. Louis, Missouri, she grew up in Arkansas. She worked as a singer, a dancer and an actress, and she was the first Black woman to work on the cable cars in San Francisco. Angelou also worked for civil rights with leaders like Martin Luther King Jr. In 1993, she read her poem On the Pulse of Morning at President Bill Clinton's inauguration. She was a professor at Wake Forest University for about thirty years. She died in 2014, at the age of 86.

Who Is Angela Davis?

Angela Davis is an American activist, professor and writer. For more than fifty years, she has worked against racism and for women's rights and prison reform. She has written many books, including Women, Race and Class.

Born in Birmingham, Alabama, Davis grew up in a time when many Black families faced racism and violence. As a teenager, she organized study groups with Black and white students. Later, she studied philosophy and became a university teacher. In 1970, she was accused of helping in a deadly escape attempt at a courtroom. She spent about 18 months in jail, but in 1972 a jury found her not guilty. She taught at the University of California, Santa Cruz, until 2008, and she still gives talks about race, prisons and women's rights.

Who Is Ketanji Brown Jackson?

Ketanji Brown Jackson is a judge in the United States. In 2022, she became the first Black woman to serve on the Supreme Court, the highest court in the country. President Joe Biden chose her, and the Senate voted to confirm her on April 7, 2022.

Born in Washington, D.C., Jackson grew up in Miami, Florida. In high school, she was class president and a champion in speech and debate. She studied at Harvard University and Harvard Law School. Before the Supreme Court, she worked as a public defender, a lawyer who helps people who cannot pay, and as a federal judge. Her first name, Ketanji Onyika, means “lovely one.” She is married to Patrick Jackson, and they have two daughters.
